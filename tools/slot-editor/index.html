  • Pick a target, then click 2 points for a line or a polygon for the ROI.
  • Drag the handles to adjust.
  • Import the slot file, open the images, click spaces: free → taken → unsure.
  • ← → previous / next image.
  • Wheel or pinch zooms; drag pans; F fits.
